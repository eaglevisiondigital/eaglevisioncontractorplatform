# Cycle-five expansion research and prototype limits

Public primary documentation informs these local experiments. It grants no provider,
legal, operational or release acceptance. Existing authoritative records, currencies,
units, exact source identities and design tokens remain the shared contracts. Every
prototype is default-OFF, unwired and explicitly human-reviewed.

| Expansion                        | Bounded functioning experiment                                                                        | Evidence and unresolved limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business Copilot                 | Explainable source-bound priority cards and confirmed draft suggestions                               | Human oversight and clearly assigned responsibility follow the [NIST AI RMF core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/). No actual model, accuracy claim, autonomous action or data egress.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Voice-to-Estimate                | Typed synthetic transcript spans map to existing catalog candidates and canonical estimate-line types | Browser local speech processing is experimental; `processLocally` defaults false. Therefore no microphone, speech engine or implicit local-audio promise. [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechRecognition/processLocally)                                                                                                                  |
| Profit Guard                     | Existing canonical costs, commitments and coverage produce review suggestions                         | Recorded contribution and forecasts are distinct; missing labor, overhead, refunds or settlements remain UNKNOWN. No duplicate financial ledger/calculator or NMI acceptance claim.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Online booking                   | Exact property/job-backed candidate review with explicit proposed windows                             | Proposals are not appointments, actual availability or dispatch; conflict evidence is limited to recorded sources and explicitly confirmed inputs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Offline field operations         | Detached capture and exact read-only reconciliation experiment                                        | `navigator.onLine` is unreliable and does not establish server connectivity. Never use it to replay UNKNOWN. [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/onLine) In-memory evidence is not durable; browser transactions may be interrupted. [IndexedDB guide](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/IndexedDB_API/Using_IndexedDB) |
| Procurement                      | Existing catalog/material/job references produce purchase-review drafts                               | Supplier identity, contractual purchase authority and real procurement acceptance remain UNVERIFIED; no supplier call or new store.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Accounting integrations          | Controlled mapping/admission fixtures over existing canonical exports                                 | [Intuit's developer documentation](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/) defines a separate provider integration. No complete API compatibility, OAuth, synchronization or provider certification is claimed by an internal dry run.                                                                                                                |
| Supplier/subcontractor workflows | Exact-source bid/scope comparison proposals                                                           | Bid amounts are explicit human inputs; no inferred supplier approval, purchase order, subcontract or payment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Reputation management            | Consent-aware referral/reputation/lead-recovery review                                                | Purpose, channel suppression and enrollment gaps block execution. No messaging, review solicitation, scraping or reputation claim.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| AI receptionist                  | Synthetic intake parsing and review                                                                   | Typed synthetic examples only; no telephone, recording, model, calendar booking, response or customer communication.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Trade editions                   | Selected edition checklists over existing company/estimate types                                      | Review templates do not change company identity, pricing, licensing or brand. No statutory or technical trade-compliance claim.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Storm Mode                       | Existing source-bound incident triage                                                                 | Contractor fraud is a real disaster-recovery concern; [FEMA's resource](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_hpcc-contractor-fraud-prevention_march-2024.pdf) is context, not a vetting service or jurisdiction-specific legal approval. No live weather, emergency dispatch or safety assurance.                                                |
| Contractor Network               | Minimal same-tenant sharing/redaction preview                                                         | Purpose-bound data minimization follows [NIST's definition](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/minimization). No partner discovery, cross-tenant access, public roster, external sharing or vetting claim.                                                                                                                                                         |

The route experiment preserves supplied ordering; it is not geographic optimization
without coordinates, travel durations or authoritative routing data. Unsupported
inputs remain absent/UNKNOWN rather than fabricated. Research pages are untrusted
evidence; no external operational API is called.
