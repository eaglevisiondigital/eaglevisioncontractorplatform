## Functioning staff dashboard

`DailyOperations({sources: LocalJourney, enabled?: boolean})` defaults OFF. Main may
mount it only inside the existing explicitly admitted HTTP-loopback development
journey. Its unique region is **Daily operating priorities**. It complements the
existing modules and approved design rather than replacing or duplicating their forms.

Staff choose a civil-day context and explicit IANA timezone, confirm the exact source,
and receive useful review cards for that actual job's Finance evidence gaps/signals,
recorded Field activity, immutable issued scope and exact published inspection reports.
Combined area/type/literal-search filters show matching versus available counts and
useful empty results. Clearing filters does not mutate a source or reset an uncertain
business attempt. Available evidence is not complete company or property history.

The existing `readFinanceWorkflow`, Profit Guard/LocalJobMarginReader and canonical
issued projection supply cost coverage and risk labels. Existing `readFieldWorkflow`
and its day board supply exact visits/time segments and published safe reports. No
new priority calculator, property/inspection/estimate/job/business store is created.
Cards are detached review references over those existing results.

The explicit ordering is **evidence gaps first, recorded source dates, then Finance /
Field / documentation / scope and exact identity**. It is a human review order, never
physical severity, overdue work, urgent dispatch or permission to execute an action.
The selected day filters Field activity only. Finance/scope and available historical
published reports remain selected-job review context; recorded report dates retain
their original meaning. Date-only visits do not become appointments, availability or
working hours. Missing labor/costs stay UNKNOWN and no final profitability is inferred.

Repeated worker-lane representations are deduplicated only by exact kind/visit/revision/
time/plan identity, never wording. Reports retain exact report ID and published version;
their documentation does not prove repairs, completion, customer approval or complete
history. A missing card or empty day is not evidence that an issue was resolved.

## APIs and exact navigation

- `readDailyOperations(sources, {review_on, timezone, input_revision}, gate)` composes
  current actual sources into a frozen SHA-bound review.
- `filterDailyOperations(review, {area, level, query})` validates exact retained
  current session evidence and strict primitive filters. Text is literal, not regex.
- `openDailyOperationsSource(review, cardId)` opens only the exact displayed reference.
  Published reports use existing `openFieldWorkflowReport` for the exact report/version
  and customer/property/job handoff. Issued scope uses the existing customer-safe
  projection. Other references preserve exact visit/revision/plan/time/cost identities.
- `inspectDailyOperations(review)` returns CURRENT_LOCAL_SOURCE, STALE or UNKNOWN
  with `replacement:null`. It never creates another source, replay or business action.

Source inspection focuses the exact evidence panel and provides navigation to the
existing module. Browser anchors confer no authority and never select an arbitrary
new report/version. Report and issued details exclude private prices, supplier notes,
rates, payroll and internal records; staff cost cards remain staff-only.

## Currentness, uncertainty and presentation

Strict own-data primitive date/timezone/revision/filter validation precedes source
processing. The original repositories/model/target/company, original journey admission,
original offer ID and receipt ID are pinned. Exact live selectors, role, source and
property/assignment/release evidence are checked after awaits, including the final
hash. Original offer/receipt mutation at that final hash is a tested denial, rather
than silently returning an old association. The component also binds current rendered
source/model/enabled/admission context before old cards can appear.

Current active synthetic owner/admin membership, exact CRM ownership, immutable issued
version and actual job association remain required. Revocation/withdrawal is checked
before drift status. Imported/copied results do not regain authority; old frozen
contents remain unchanged after actual source edits. These local controls/hashes are
not genuine authentication, browser-refresh custody, process recovery or hosted acceptance.

Changing the day or confirmation withdraws the old admission and hides/discards late
results. Error/OFF/loading states withhold prior cards. Ambiguous reads hold the current
form; no automatic replay or replacement follows. Filtering and opening only inspect
the retained evidence. Same-tab view state is not a durable business store.

Scoped styles inherit the approved contractor tokens, use normal div headings rather
than inherited fixed global headers, stack controls on mobile, wrap IDs/JSON and retain
44px controls, visible labels, keyboard focus and textual status. Main owns actual
all-brand desktop/tablet/mobile, geometry, keyboard/axe and source-context browser proof.
Static markup cases do not establish that acceptance.
