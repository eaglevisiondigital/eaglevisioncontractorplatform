## Functioning isolated implementation

`readProfitGuardReview` composes the existing `LocalJobMarginReader.readCompletion`
and canonical `jobFinancials`. It produces an immutable internal review with exact
organization, job revision, issued version, estimate, customer and recorded property
references; existing customer/CRM authorization remains in that reader. Null recorded
property remains null; no property relationship is invented. All three approved sample
companies are supported. The module is unmounted and defaults OFF; explicit original
`enabled:true` and `confirmed:true` admission and current synthetic owner/admin
membership are required. It rejects connected sources before financial reads.

Useful risk cards identify canonical unpaid purchase commitments, forecast/commitment
review overlap, category overruns, margin erosion, negative contribution, incomplete
cost coverage and missing overhead/provider evidence. Amounts and thresholds are
copied from existing validated calculations. Exact approved expense, review, source
line and credit-adjustment IDs remain available through the allowlisted completion
review. No supplier matching, cost engine, ledger, policy, document, job or business
store is added. Forecast remains additive; the module does not silently net it against
commitments or count an approved payment/credit twice. Internal recorded payment and
credit kinds never establish a bank payment, provider refund, void or settlement.

The result contains a SHA-256 content hash, a frozen detached comparison and a
session-only read-only verification association. The final exact synchronous
`Phase3Repository.syntheticJob` checkpoint runs after all asynchronous capture/hash
waits, followed by the original admission and current membership check. A real job
revision change or staff revocation during the last hash wait denies the result.
Verification returns `CURRENT_JOB_SOURCE`, `STALE` or `UNKNOWN`, always with
`replacement:null`. Copied/rehydrated evidence has no association and remains UNKNOWN;
uncertainty never starts a writer, replacement identity or replay. This association
is not a persistent business store, durable custody or genuine authentication.

## Time capture and overhead proposals remain blocked

An optional explicit human time proposal selects an actual worker from current
sample-company relationships and validates the proposed work interval against the
existing captured time entries using the unchanged `validateTime`. It rejects wrong
or revoked membership, ambiguous worker IDs, overlapping intervals, missing timezone
offsets, invalid lengths and invalid job/overhead combinations. No time entry ID is
created in the authoritative repository. No writer or wage calculator is called.

The existing workforce reader is asynchronous; it cannot provide an atomic final
snapshot of new unposted time or worker changes. This limitation is retained rather
than promoted into acceptance. Two captured workforce footprints must match, but all
proposals are explicitly `BLOCKED_DRAFT_ONLY` / `CAPTURED_NOT_ATOMIC`, with current
worker/time coverage UNKNOWN and monetary allocation null. A typed overhead label is
human input, not an approved overhead center or allocation policy. The review also
shows whether its captured interval overlaps a locked payroll range, without claiming
permission to post. Subsequent exact reads can report changed evidence STALE; they
never make the proposal executable. `CURRENT_JOB_SOURCE` specifically excludes current
workforce coverage. A hash demonstrates content fidelity only.

No rates, payroll deductions, supplier names, private expense/review notes, workforce
names or raw existing time tasks appear in the output. The proposed task is the
reviewer's explicit internal input, not extracted private source text. The result is
staff-only and must never be passed into customer-safe projections. Existing locked
labor IDs are evidence references, not an assertion of payroll payment or availability.
