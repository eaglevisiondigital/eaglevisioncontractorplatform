## Working local increment

`FinanceDesk({sources: LocalJourney, enabled?: boolean})` defaults OFF and uses the
original actual journey instance. Main alone mounts it inside the existing explicit
HTTP-loopback development gate. Its unique region is **Finance operating review**.
It complements the existing Time/Materials/Invoice review rather than reproducing
its forms or replacing the approved back office/customer portal.

Staff enter explicit additional remaining-cost proposals in the existing five cost
categories. Every amount, including zero, is explicit. A button may copy the actual
saved forecast as a human-selected starting point. A separate checkbox attests that
these forecasts exclude recorded costs and unpaid commitments. A final confirmation
admits the exact saved source and unsaved proposal; editing withdraws it immediately.
This statement does not independently verify that a human forecast avoids every
real-world overlap or establish an approved forecast, purchase or labor plan.

`readFinanceDesk(repo, target, {job_review, remaining,
excludes_unpaid_commitments:true, input_revision}, gate)` reuses the original
`readJobReview` → Finance workflow → Profit Guard → LocalJobMarginReader composition.
The issued estimate/customer/property/job/currency/revision, offer and actual test
receipt remain exact. It never selects whichever estimate/version happens to be newest.

On a detached copy of the actual job, only `projection.remaining` is replaced by the
human proposal; it is not added to the saved remaining forecast. Actual expenses,
credit relationships, approved reviews, net unpaid commitments, issued baseline and
source thresholds remain byte-identical. `costs_complete` is conservatively false.
Existing `projectLocalJobMargin` and `projectJobCostCompletion` supply all arithmetic,
cost-to-complete, contribution, ratios and overrun/erosion warnings. No second calculator,
job, estimate, ledger or financial store exists. Proposals never save a forecast or
change a job, expense, commitment, customer total, management completion or payment.

Labor coverage comes only from the already verified actual reader result and its
exact entry/locked-period identities. If that source is UNKNOWN, the scenario's
aggregate actual/cost-to-complete/contribution/margin amounts remain null. It does
not substitute zero labor or use entered future labor as proof of recorded labor.
Raw proposed category amounts remain visible and useful with that limitation.
Partial coverage stays partial; contribution before unallocated overhead is not
final business profit, physical completion, settlement or permission to incur costs.

## Source, privacy and presentation boundaries

The original live target, nested proposal input, original preview/review object references,
current eligible synthetic owner/admin, CRM property, exact source and release evidence
are rechecked across awaits. Own-data/strict primitive and bounded money checks reject
accessors, coercion and serializers before identity hashing. The last await is followed
by synchronous shared source/admission/current-role checks. Immutable SHA-bound results
retain only session read associations; hashes are not authorization or durable custody.

`inspectFinanceDesk` only checks the exact retained result and returns CURRENT_LOCAL_SOURCE,
STALE or UNKNOWN with `replacement:null`. Copied evidence, changed input, revoked access
or withdrawn admission cannot create replacement evidence. Old immutable scenario
contents remain unchanged after source edits. No retry or business replay follows UNKNOWN.

The panel owns model/target/enabled/admission and current rendered source binding.
Changing any binding remounts before an old result can render. Input edits discard
late results and withdraw prior confirmation. Error/OFF/loading states withhold amounts;
ambiguous results hold the current form. These are same-tab presentation controls,
not refresh/process-loss custody or real authentication. No customer-connected route
or private cost/workforce data enters customer projections.

New styles are scoped, inherit contractor colors, provide visible labels/44px controls,
focus outlines, mobile stacking and wrapping source IDs. A normal div heading avoids
inherited fixed-height global header styles. Existing layouts/artwork/CSS stay locked.
Main owns actual three-brand desktop/tablet/mobile/axe/keyboard/context browser proof;
static render tests do not establish browser acceptance.
