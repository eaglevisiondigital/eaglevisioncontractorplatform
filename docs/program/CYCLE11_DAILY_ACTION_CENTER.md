# Cycle 11 — Daily action center

Exact parent `b6ac4fc28c5a50d2029db4a5a69d345af0e3f35b`. Local, default-OFF, same-tab synthetic staff review only.

`DailyActionCenter` extends the existing priority explanations and Business Briefing rather than replacing their source readers or preparing AI actions. Staff choose the review day, IANA timezone and one of two explicit criteria: evidence gaps first or recorded source day first. The actual briefing supplies the recorded day, joined to explanations by exact key, target, source kind/ID/version/revision and observation; dates are never parsed from prose. Deterministic review bands separate missing evidence, earlier context, review-day context, later context and undated context. Neither a review band nor a date comparison establishes operational urgency, a missed visit, an overdue obligation, physical severity or work completion.

Source facts, selected review criteria and suggestions remain visibly separate. Filters combine exact job, area, evidence class, review band and literal text with accurate available/matching counts. Original immutable report/version, issued scope, job and safe financial sources open through existing source navigation; exact job selection is a separate explicit callback. No arbitrary URL or latest-source substitution is introduced.

APIs: `readDailyActionCenter`, `filterDailyActionCenter`, `openDailyActionSource`, `openDailyActionJob` and `inspectDailyActionCenter`. Props: `{ sources: OperatingSources, enabled?: boolean = false, onSelectJob?: (jobId: string) => void }`. Main owns route composition. Strict original-input, current staff, gate, source-root and child-source checks run after asynchronous work and during reads. Copied evidence has no authority; stale or uncertain evidence remains UNKNOWN with exact read-only reconciliation and no replacement. Component context replacement hides prior results and withdraws in-flight admission. Default-OFF rendering reads no source getter.

No business priority store, duplicate job/CRM/service model, AI generation, microphone, messages, dispatch, schedule mutation, contract or financial authority is added. Projections retain source IDs while excluding contacts, supplier prices, individual workforce amounts and private notes. The component uses the shared contractor palette, scoped warm surfaces, accessible controls, touch targets, keyboard focus, mobile stacking and a named original-source region. Approved design files are unchanged.
