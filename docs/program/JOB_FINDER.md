# Loaded job finder

Optional development child of exact #545 (`2b6e2a96b9015f75a07b8224c218a4df868fac18`). The existing Jobs management view gains a collapsed finder over its already loaded job records: literal case-insensitive name/ID search, five results per page, loaded-subset counts and explicit Open job. Search and paging preserve the existing selection and unsaved forecast. No demo job state, progress, customer approval or financial totals are invented.

Opening uses the original job-selection callback only after current estimates access and the enabled estimates module are checked before and after a fresh existing-repository job read. The whole response must have unique same-tenant job identities. The selected row must still match its captured loaded contents after the final authority wait, and the repository/company/source/callback context must remain current. Missing, changed, foreign, duplicate or denied evidence withholds navigation and hides the finder results until the loaded-source context is refreshed. Detached old results cannot select a job or clear the new context's view. Errors are generic; repeated pending clicks make one read.

These are read/navigation checks, not operational Auth acceptance, a continuous idle-session guarantee or financial authority. Existing Financial job selection, JobDetail, forecast CAS/UNKNOWN holds, original-scope reader and financial callbacks remain unchanged. The finder creates no records, schema, ledger, route or command custody. Opening a different job intentionally changes the current view; users should save entered work first.

Approved references 7 and 8 guide job overview/navigation. This increment supplies partial loaded-record navigation, not the full demo cards, dark shell, lifecycle, crew scheduling, completion or financial dashboard parity. Original63 reference identities and classifications and the five locked assets/thirteen bindings remain unchanged.

Validation uses synthetic repositories and the actual sample App at desktop/tablet/mobile. Cases cover literal input, pagination and count shrink, current selection, pre-read denial, post-read withdrawal, malformed/foreign/duplicate/stale source, final-wait source change, detached company results, duplicate clicks, zero financial writes, unsaved forecast preservation, keyboard/Axe and responsive overflow. Existing scope-reader and financial browser regressions are retained. Local tests and independent technical review do not establish genuine Auth/MFA, custody, recovery or hosted acceptance.

The candidate remains isolated from the launch and held source-admission work. Release1.0 remains NO-GO / NOT READY / NOT AUTHORIZED; no SQL/grants, real tenant or merchant activation, provider/payment/message execution, recovery, merge or deployment is authorized.
